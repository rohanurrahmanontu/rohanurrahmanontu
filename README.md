## 🏆 Featured Case Study

    <td width="60%">
      <h3>Pako Stays — lead to signed contract, on autopilot</h3>
      <p>Automation backbone for a short-term rental management company in Toronto, the GTA and Panama.</p>
      <p><b>Problem →</b> Leads from two web forms copied by hand into a sheet, a CRM and a task board. Quotes took days, follow-ups got forgotten, contracts built one at a time.</p>
      <p><b>Build →</b> Three n8n workflows: instant capture with distance-aware quoting, a daily hot/warm/cold nurture engine with city-specific emails, and a one-click contract generator shipping to DocuSign.</p>
      <p><b>Result →</b> Every lead logged, quoted and welcomed via email + WhatsApp within seconds. Reps step in only when a lead goes quiet.</p>
