## Featured Case Study

### Pako Stays — lead to signed contract, on autopilot
Automation backbone for a short-term rental management company operating in Toronto, the GTA and Panama.

- **Problem:** Leads from two web forms were copied by hand into a sheet, a CRM and a task board. Quotes took days, follow-ups were forgotten, contracts were built one by one.
- **Build:** Three n8n workflows — instant capture with a distance-aware onboarding quote, a daily hot/warm/cold nurture engine with city-specific emails, and a one-click contract generator that ships to DocuSign.
- **Result:** Every lead is logged, quoted and welcomed by email and WhatsApp within seconds. Reps only step in when a lead goes quiet; signed contracts update the pipeline automatically.

`3 production workflows` · `9 integrated tools` · `45-day automated nurture` · `4 city-specific tracks`
